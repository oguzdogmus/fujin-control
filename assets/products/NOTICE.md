# Product imagery

Product images in this directory are supplied by the official GamePower product listings and remain the property of their respective rights holders. They are not covered by this repository’s MIT license. Their inclusion identifies the products in this independent controller; it does not imply endorsement or permission to reuse them for unrelated purposes.

`manifest.json` records the exact product page, original image URL, SHA-256 and review date for each of the 22 GamePower labels. Identical source images for standard/Pro pairs share an asset; no visual difference is invented. These are unchanged listing images, not photographs of the connected device or current lighting. Tinker’s source photo depicts a white model; Tinker V2 uses the official Black listing. USB IDs do not establish a color variant.
